TUBELESS SETUP IN TRUJILLO

Our tubeless setup in Trujillo is engineered to optimize pressure, traction and structural stability for MTB and road cycling.

The tubeless system operates under a physical threshold defined by the Young–Laplace criterion:

Pᵢ > (2 · γ · cosθ) / r

If internal pressure exceeds the sealant's cohesion capacity at the critical pore radius, sealing integrity is compromised.

  • Internal rim preparation
  • Tubeless tape applied under calibrated tension
  • Valves sealed and fixed for structural integrity
  • Precise sealant dosing based on actual tire volume
  • Proper bead seating and pressure verification

We use Stan's sealant because it meets measurable stability and rheological performance criteria under real pressure and temperature variation.

Real benefits

  • Fewer punctures
  • Greater pressure stability
  • Improved traction at lower PSI
  • Reduced rotational weight
