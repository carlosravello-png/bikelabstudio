FAQ_Protocol // Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions_

How long does general maintenance take?

The full protocol requires 2 to 4 hours of uninterrupted technical work depending on the bike's wear condition. At BikeLab Studio, Trujillo, same-day delivery is completed in most cases.

Which components are serviced?

The protocol includes full drivetrain disassembly, deep cleaning with technical degreaser, brake calibration for Shimano, SRAM or Magura systems, derailleur adjustment with precision tension tools, component-specific lubrication per manufacturer specs, and certified torque re-tightening at all critical points.

How often is general maintenance recommended?

For regular use on dirt or paved routes, we recommend general maintenance every 3 to 4 months. Under intensive conditions — racing, rain, dust — the optimal cycle is every 6 to 8 weeks. Our Trujillo workshop evaluates each bike's actual condition before issuing a diagnosis.

Does it apply to high-end MTB and road bikes?

Yes. We work with hardtail and full-suspension MTB, road and gravel bikes. We service high-end components including Shimano XTR, SRAM XX, Campagnolo Super Record and carbon groupsets. The protocol adapts to each system's technical specifications.
