"New" does not mean "optimal." 47 units prove it with numbers.

A bicycle fresh out of the box arrives with a factory mechanical state that is almost never the optimal one. This dataset diagnoses 47 new bicycles (pre-use) across five failure vectors, each with its quantified impact in watts, braking distance or tolerance deviation. It is the basis of the pre-use diagnostic framework (PDI).

Module_02 // Reading
→ A new bike "loses" between 3.6 and 8.0 W recoverable in drivetrain alone, poorly lubricated from the factory. → ~40% of the bolts come out of the specified torque range — a real risk in carbon frames. → Spokes with ±20–30% tension variation: accelerated fatigue from the first month. → Braking can come out +25% longer (+1.75 m at 30 km/h) due to factory air/hydraulic compliance.
Module_03 // Reserved Method

The framework and the measurement protocols are public in the white paper with its DOI. What is not published openly is the per-unit dataset (the 47 individual records with their raw readings). We reveal the vectors and their aggregated impact; the per-unit detail is shared under agreement.

Module_05 // Limitations and Access

The sample (47 units) is illustrative of the population of new mid/high-range bicycles; it does not claim population-level statistical validity but rather to quantify real vectors. It allows expansion with more units. Provisional by design.
