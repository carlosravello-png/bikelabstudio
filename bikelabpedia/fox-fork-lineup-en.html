The Fox fork lineup explained

Quick answer

Fox names by stanchion diameter: 32 (XC), 34 (trail), 36 (enduro), 38 (gravity), 40 (DH). On top comes the finish, from lowest to highest: Rhythm → Performance → Performance Elite → Factory (with Kashima). Cartridges: GRIP, FIT4 and GRIP2. In equivalences, Factory ≈ RockShox Ultimate; compare them in RockShox vs Fox and look at the travel your frame allows.

The number is the discipline; the finish is how much you spend on adjustments and stanchions. Moving up a finish doesn't change the discipline: a 34 Factory is still a trail fork.

The number: stanchion diameter and discipline

32. The thinnest, for cross country: low weight and short travel.

34. Trail: a touch more stiffness without putting on weight.

36. Enduro: thick stanchions for demanding terrain.

38. Gravity: even burlier, for heavy enduro.

40. Downhill: the biggest stanchion, 180-200 mm of travel.

The finish: from Rhythm to Factory

The finish steps up in this order: Rhythm → Performance → Performance Elite → Factory. The Factory carries the stanchions with Kashima (gold) coating and the most complete adjustments. With the level, the damper cartridge also changes: GRIP, FIT4 and GRIP2.

How to choose without going wrong

First the number, according to your discipline and the travel your frame allows; then the finish, according to budget. A 34 works for trail in both Rhythm and Factory: what changes is the fine tuning and the weight, not the discipline. Before buying, confirm what fork fits your bike.

Common mistake: thinking "Factory" fixes a fork that's too thin for your use. If you ride enduro, a 32 Factory is still XC: you need to step up a number (36), not just a finish.

Frequently asked questions

What do 32, 34, 36, 38 mean?

The stanchion diameter and the discipline: 32 XC, 34 trail, 36 enduro, 38 gravity, 40 DH. The higher the number, the burlier.

Rhythm vs Factory?

They are finishes, from lowest to highest: Rhythm → Performance → Performance Elite → Factory (with Kashima). It steps up adjustments and cartridge.

What does Factory match?

Factory ≈ RockShox Ultimate; Performance Elite ≈ Select+; Performance ≈ Select. It's a tier equivalence.
