Bike geometry

Why aren't two "size M" bikes the same bike? Why does one feel like it's on rails and another twitchy at the same head angle? Here's all of geometry from scratch — and, what almost nobody explains, what you feel when each number changes. The encyclopedia of consequences, not definitions.
