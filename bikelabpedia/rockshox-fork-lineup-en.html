The RockShox fork lineup explained

Quick answer

The RockShox lineup runs from less to more: Recon (XC) → 35 → Pike (trail) → Lyrik (enduro) → ZEB (DH/heavy enduro). The cartridge steps up from Motion Control (mid range) to Charger/Charger 3 (high end), and the air spring is DebonAir. To get it right, look at your discipline and the travel your frame allows; if you're torn against Fox, compare in RockShox vs Fox.

It's not "pricier = better for you": it's more travel and more burliness for more aggressive disciplines. Fitting a ZEB on an XC bike is as much a mistake as a Recon on enduro.

The order of the lineup, from XC to DH

Recon. The entry point for cross country and light trail. Short travel (80-120 mm), contained weight and just enough to ride firm.

35. The intermediate step: burlier stanchions than the Recon, made for trail without going to the price of a Pike.

Pike. The trail benchmark. Balance between weight and stiffness, typical travel of 120-140 mm.

Lyrik. The enduro one. Thicker stanchions and 150-170 mm of travel for demanding terrain.

ZEB. The burliest: heavy enduro and downhill, with big stanchions and 180-200 mm of travel.

What changes as you move up

Two things rise with the model: the stanchion diameter (more stiffness for rougher terrain) and the damper cartridge, which goes from Motion Control in the mid range to Charger or Charger 3 at the high end, with better compression and rebound control. The family's air spring is DebonAir. Before choosing, confirm what fork fits your bike.

Common mistake: buying the most expensive fork "just in case". If your frame is trail, a heavy-enduro ZEB weighs too much and changes the geometry. Choose by discipline and travel, not by price.

Frequently asked questions

In what order do they go?

Recon (XC) → 35 → Pike (trail) → Lyrik (enduro) → ZEB (DH). The further down, the more travel and burliness.

Motion Control vs Charger?

They are damper cartridges: Motion Control is mid range, Charger and Charger 3 are high end with better control.

Which one do I pick?

By discipline and frame travel: Recon XC, Pike trail, Lyrik enduro, ZEB DH. Don't fit more travel than your bike allows.
