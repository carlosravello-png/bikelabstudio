DW-link vs VPP vs Maestro: they're not the same

Quick answer

The internet keeps repeating that they're "the same thing because they have two links". False. The VPP rotates its links in opposite directions (counter-rotating); the DW-link and the Maestro in the same direction (co-rotating), but with different geometries. That changes the anti-squat, the axle path and the character. Three cousins, not three clones.

It's mistake #1 in bike reviews. Here we settle it once and for all, with what really sets them apart.

The one thing you have to remember

Look at how the two links rotate. If they turn opposite to each other, it's VPP. If they turn the same way, it's DW-link or Maestro (and there the position of the pivots is what separates them). That single observation puts you ahead of 95% of reviews.

Important: none is "the best". They're characters. And within each family, the specific tune of one model can differ more than two families do from each other. Comparing by "family" without looking at the curve of the specific model is the second most common mistake.

Frequently asked questions

Are they the same?

No: VPP is counter-rotating; DW-link and Maestro are co-rotating with different geometries. That changes anti-squat, axle path and character.

Which is best?

None in the abstract. What decides it is the tune of the specific model, not the family.
