Preventive maintenance under wear control criteria

Every mechanical system experiences progressive wear during operation.

V = k · (W · L) / H

Archard's wear law.

V = wear volume

W = applied load

L = sliding distance

H = material hardness

Preventive maintenance reduces friction, contamination and unnecessary load to slow cumulative wear.

We do not repair major failures here.
We prevent them.

Preventive maintenance protocol

  • General cleaning of drivetrain and exposed components
  • Chain lubrication and critical point lubrication
  • Basic shifting adjustment
  • Basic brake adjustment
  • Tire pressure verification
  • Visual inspection of drivetrain wear
  • Quick check of headset and general bolt tightness

Light intervention.
Functional preservation.

Friction control and efficiency

Ff = μN

Frequently Asked Questions_

How does it differ from general maintenance?

Preventive maintenance is a functional intervention for bikes in good mechanical condition. It focuses on clean drivetrain, chain lubrication, basic brake and derailleur adjustment, and pressure check. General maintenance involves complete disassembly and full system recalibration.

How often is preventive maintenance recommended?

Every 4 to 6 weeks for regular cyclists in Trujillo. In dusty or occasionally rainy conditions, the ideal interval is every 3 to 4 weeks. Regular preventive service significantly extends the service life of chain, cassette and chainrings.

Does it include brake and derailleur inspection?

Yes. The protocol includes brake and shift cable adjustment, lever travel verification, front and rear derailleur cleaning, and pivot point lubrication. Replacement cables, housing or brake pads are quoted separately if the diagnosis requires them.
