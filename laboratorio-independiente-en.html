Module_02 // The_Document_You_Need

The Product Manager doesn't need convincing. They need the document that shows their boss why the diagnosis is trustworthy.

A press test is an opinion with a photo. An in-house test has a declared conflict of interest. A white paper with a DOI is something else: citable, permanent, indexed on OpenAIRE, reproducible by any engineer with access to the same conditions. It doesn't disappear. It can't be edited. It has no commercial agenda.

That document is what we produce.

Module_05 // Testing_Axes

Five areas with methodology already documented:

TYRES & PRESSURE Constant-deflection model. Cross-validation SILCA / Rene Herse. Correction for real internal width. Front/rear coupling. MTB SUSPENSION Dual-chamber polytropic. Inverse calibration by brand and model. Leverage ratio, temperature, nitrogen variation. DRIVETRAIN Per-sprocket friction losses. Efficiency under real load. Energy and economic cost per kilometre. HYDRAULIC BRAKES Fade thermodynamics. DOT vs mineral behaviour. High-altitude analysis (>3,000 m). Pads and rotors. TRIBOLOGY & LUBRICATION Chain wear under specific contaminants. Galvanic corrosion between dissimilar materials. Greases and lubricants in local climate conditions.

Module_07 // Collaboration_Protocol

You describe the component and the goal of the study.

No form. No bureaucracy.

You receive the draft white paper for technical review.
