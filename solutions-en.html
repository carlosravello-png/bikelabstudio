A bicycle isn't tuned. It's interrogated.

The exterior lies. The paint, the groupset, the logo on the down tube: alibis. The truth lives where light doesn't reach —inside a hub, at the pad-rotor interface, in the chain no one cleaned in time—. Shadows scare most people. That's where we work.

Disassembly is the first act of honesty: separating every part until the wear confesses what the owner never knew. There is no "feel": there is torque. There is no "good enough": there is measured tolerance. A poorly adjusted bearing doesn't warn you and doesn't hurt —it just destroys, silently, kilometer after kilometer.

These are the case files: each one a closed case. The symptom, the real cause, the procedure. Diagnosis by elimination, not intuition; so your bicycle stops being wasted engineering.

You can copy the black and the shadows. Not the judgment that orders them.
