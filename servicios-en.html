Module_00.1 // Technical Context

Cycling across Peru? Check your bike at BikeLab Studio before continuing your route.

BikeLab Studio provides technical bicycle service for long-distance cyclists, bike travelers, MTB riders and road cyclists. We perform drivetrain adjustment, bearing inspection, technical lubrication and certified torque control on every critical component.

We use professional bearing extraction and press tools, SKF LGHP 2/1 grease for hubs and axial components, Motorex 2000 for headset service and Liqui-Moly copper paste for titanium and aluminum interfaces.

If you bought a new bicycle, bring it to BikeLab Studio for a professional mechanical setup to ensure the drivetrain, bearings and torque points meet the manufacturer's technical standard.

Our service structure is based on fixed prices. Every rate reflects premium technical consumables, specialized tools and real mechanical labor time.

Module_01 // Frequently Asked Questions

How much does general bicycle maintenance cost?

General maintenance is priced at S/ 90. It includes full disassembly, cleaning of all components, chain and drivetrain lubrication, derailleur adjustment, brake adjustment and general calibration. Typical turnaround: 24 to 48 hours.

How much does a hydraulic brake bleed cost?

Hydraulic brake bleeding starts at S/ 40 per brake. The service includes hydraulic pressure restoration, lever and caliper inspection, and bite point verification. Compatible with Shimano, SRAM, Magura and Tektro brakes.

What does MTB suspension service include and how much does it cost?

The service includes full internal disassembly, chamber cleaning, seal and oil replacement, and positive pressure and rebound calibration. Price ranges from S/ 70 for basic forks to S/ 130 for high-performance rear shocks.

How do I book an appointment at BikeLab Studio?

You can book directly via WhatsApp at +51 934 391 545. Send your name, bike or component model, the service you need, and your available day and time range for drop-off. We respond within 2 hours during workshop hours.
