PROTOCOLO_00 // WHO WE ARE

Bicycle Workshop in Trujillo_

BikeLab Studio is not a conventional bike shop. It is a technical bicycle workshop in Trujillo where maintenance is executed as applied engineering: structural diagnostics, torque control, friction analysis, material fatigue assessment, and tolerance verification.

No improvisation. Measured, verified, documented.

Bicycles do not fail by chance. They fail due to undetected wear, incorrect lubrication, invisible contamination, or out-of-spec torque. Our role is to identify those variables before they become failure.

The standard is simple: If it cannot be explained precisely, it is not executed. If it cannot be verified, it is not released.
