Frequently Asked Questions_

How long does wheel truing take?

Standard truing takes 20 to 40 minutes per wheel depending on the degree of misalignment. If the wheel shows severe tension irregularity or loose spokes, the process is extended to ensure full uniformity. At BikeLab Studio, Trujillo, we use a calibrated spoke tension meter on every intervention.

When do I need my wheels trued?

Clear signs: brake rub at one point in the rotation cycle, vibration while riding, visible lateral deformation, or after a lateral impact on trail. We recommend preventive truing every 6 months for MTB bikes with intensive trail use, where spokes work under constant lateral load.

What is the difference between standard truing and the disassembly service?

Standard truing corrects alignment without removing the tire, working from the rim's exterior. The disassembly and reassembly service includes complete tire removal, allowing internal rim inspection, spoke base cleaning from the inside and in-depth nipple verification.
