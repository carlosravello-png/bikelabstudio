How to cite_

Naming the source and the author, and linking to the original, is enough. Suggested format:

Ravello Joo, C. E. (2026). [Article or dataset title]. BikeLab Studio.
https://www.bikelabstudio.com/[url] · CC BY 4.0

For calculators and datasets with a DOI, cite the DOI. It is the cleanest and most permanent form:

Ravello Joo, C. E. (2026). BikeLab SAG Calculator. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20672790 · CC BY-NC 4.0

If you publish on the web, the link to the original must be crawlable (no rel="nofollow" added to hide the source).
